# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends playing a casual co-op and racing party game together: on phones held in landscape (both thumbs busy with the joystick and TOOT) and on laptops (hands on WASD and Space). Most UI is touched mid-run, so every in-game control has to work in under a second without looking away from the river.

## Product Purpose

Toot Together is a browser party game where hippos on a raft steer by tooting. Success is a room of friends laughing together: the UI should get out of the way of the run and make talking to each other, picking a raft and starting again effortless.

## Brand Personality

Chunky, cheeky, toy-like. Thick ink outlines, candy colours, a bouncy display font, everything feeling like a plastic toy you could pick up. References: Fall Guys (bubbly, colourful, readable at a glance) and the Valorant ping wheel (structure and speed of in-game comms).

## Anti-references

- Browser-native or system-looking controls: unicode arrows and checkmarks instead of the game's own icons, default form widgets.
- Flat, thin, corporate UI that would look at home in a SaaS dashboard.
- Panels that grow with player count and push the game out of view.

## Design Principles

1. **Never make a player look away.** In-game UI is glanceable, fixed in place, and driven by muscle memory (the same thing always lives in the same spot).
2. **Thumbs and keys first.** Every action has a fast path for a held thumb and a held key; tapping through menus is the fallback.
3. **The game's own toy box.** Use the game's icon set, ink outlines and display font; add to them rather than reaching for glyphs or defaults.
4. **Scale to a full room.** Anything listing players stays tidy with 16 hippos on 4 rafts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Rafts are told apart by shape as well as colour. Touch targets at least 44 px; no in-game text under 13 px.
